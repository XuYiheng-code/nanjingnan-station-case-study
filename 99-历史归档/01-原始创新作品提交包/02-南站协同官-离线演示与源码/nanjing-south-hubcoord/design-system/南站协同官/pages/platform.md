# Platform page override

The generated dashboard baseline is subordinate to the existing HubCoord visual language.

- Palette: deep navy `#07131f`, paper `#f2eee4`, teal `#156a73`, bronze `#b18645`, warning rust `#b95935`.
- Typography: local CJK serif/sans fallbacks only. The competition demo must remain readable offline; do not load Google Fonts.
- Layout: editorial case dossier with data-dense drill-down sections. Square borders and paper texture are intentional; do not convert cards to generic rounded SaaS panels.
- Motion: short color and opacity feedback only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Accessibility: 44px interactive targets, visible `:focus-visible`, persistent mobile anchor navigation, semantic tables/details, and text labels in addition to color.
- Data display: approved evidence, research-review conflicts, simulation settings, and measured test results must remain visually distinct.
